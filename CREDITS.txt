HOLDOUT - prototype build (build 59)

Characters, outfits and animations:
- Quaternius - Universal Base Characters, Universal Animation Library 1 & 2,
  Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy (Ranger). All CC0 1.0 (public domain). https://quaternius.com
Engine: three.js r128 (MIT), loaded from CDN.

To play: open index.html from a web host (itch.io HTML5 upload, Netlify, GitHub Pages).
Opening the file straight from your computer won't load the character - browsers block that.
Co-op only works on the Claude artifact link.


WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump/vault · Left click attack (hold = heavy) · Right click aim/block
V roll · C crouch · E use · Q swap · I bag · M map · P pause
Menu has a MAP switch: Desert City (play) or Movement Test Area.
Phones/tablets: left thumb moves, right thumb looks, buttons on the right. Controllers work too.

Guns: Ultimate Gun Pack by Quaternius (CC0) - pistol, revolver, shotgun, SMG, assault rifle, sniper.
R = reload (keyboard). Controller/touch: press X / USE with nothing to interact with.

Build 67 controls: Hotbar 1 melee, 2 sidearm, 3 primary, 4 light, 5 bandage, 6 medkit, 7 pills, 8 food (number keys, tap, or hold Y + D-pad left/right).
B = instant roll, R3 = crouch (R3 = switch shoulder while aiming). Settings > INPUT: touch buttons AUTO/ALWAYS/OFF, dodge INSTANT/CLASSIC, crosshair size.
Characters: Ultimate Modular Men Pack by Quaternius (CC0), re-skinned onto the Ranger skeleton.

Finger grip poses measured from "FPS Rig AKM" and "FPS Rig" by J-Toastie (Poly Pizza). Only pose angles were used; no model files are included.

- "The Wrapped" PS1 horror monster by Codyanka (https://codyanka.itch.io/the-wrapped), CC0 public domain. Used for The Twisted (runner).
